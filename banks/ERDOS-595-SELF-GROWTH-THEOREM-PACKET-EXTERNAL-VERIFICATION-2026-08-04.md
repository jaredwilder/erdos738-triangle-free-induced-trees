## 2. Definitions

All graphs are simple and may be infinite.

A **triangle-free edge cover** of a graph \(G\) is a family of triangle-free
spanning subgraphs whose edge sets cover \(E(G)\).

The **triangle-cover number**
\[
tc(G)
\]
is the least cardinality of such a cover. Equivalently, it is the least number
of colors in an edge-coloring of \(G\) with no monochromatic triangle.

A hypothetical **Erdős #595 witness** is a \(K_4\)-free graph \(G\) satisfying
\[
tc(G)>\aleph_0.
\]

For an edge set \(F\subseteq E(G)\), \(G-F\) denotes the spanning graph obtained
by deleting \(F\). For \(S\subseteq V(G)\), \(G-S\) is the induced graph on
\(V(G)\setminus S\).

## D0 — Cover–Partition Equivalence

**Status:** `UNCONDITIONAL_ELEMENTARY`  
**Claim hash:** `ab06378e76fdab6b2f10bad6db6b0aa2bd261fea2c8198c0bc01fa9a58a710a6`  
**Lean mission:** `triangleCover_cover_partition_equiv`  
**Novelty query:** `"triangle-free edge cover" partition equivalence least layer`

### Statement

For every graph \(G\) and cardinal \(\lambda\), the following are equivalent:

1. \(E(G)\) is covered by \(\lambda\) triangle-free subgraphs;
2. \(E(G)\) is partitioned into \(\lambda\) triangle-free edge sets;
3. there is an edge-coloring \(c:E(G)\to\lambda\) with no monochromatic triangle.

### Proof

The implications (2)\(\Leftrightarrow\)(3) are definitional. A partition is a cover, so (2)\(\Rightarrow\)(1). For (1)\(\Rightarrow\)(2), well-order the \(\lambda\) covering layers and assign each edge to the least layer containing it. Each new layer is a subgraph of an original triangle-free layer and is therefore triangle-free.

### Exact falsifier

A triangle-free cover for which least-layer assignment creates a triangle.

## T1 — The κ-Complete Triangle-Cover Ideal

**Status:** `UNCONDITIONAL_ELEMENTARY`  
**Claim hash:** `0d932677571443ad09574af611c80611fe06e8ecfee7497986531c66a72bf4ad`  
**Lean mission:** `triangleCoverIdeal_kappaComplete`  
**Novelty query:** `"κ-complete ideal" triangle-free edge cover`

### Statement

Let \(\kappa\) be an infinite cardinal and \(G\) a graph. Define
\[
\mathcal I_\kappa(G)=
\{F\subseteq E(G): tc((V(G),F))\le \kappa\}.
\]
Then \(\mathcal I_\kappa(G)\) is downward closed and closed under unions of at most \(\kappa\) members.

### Proof

Downward closure follows because every subgraph of a triangle-free graph is triangle-free. Let \(\{F_i:i\in I\}\subseteq\mathcal I_\kappa(G)\) with \(|I|\le\kappa\). For each \(i\), cover \(F_i\) by at most \(\kappa\) triangle-free layers. All these layers together number at most \(|I|\cdot\kappa\le\kappa\), because \(\kappa\) is infinite. They cover \(\bigcup_iF_i\).

### Exact falsifier

A family of at most κ edge sets, each κ-coverable, whose union is not κ-coverable.

## T2 — Null-Edge Deletion Persistence

**Status:** `UNCONDITIONAL_ELEMENTARY`  
**Claim hash:** `71823612fc62acb81d98b98ac699a1bc54be35f549387390a7fcc86aa1c1758d`  
**Lean mission:** `triangleCover_gt_delete_nullEdges`  
**Novelty query:** `"triangle cover number" deletion persistence edge ideal`

### Statement

Let \(\kappa\) be infinite. If \(tc(G)>\kappa\) and \(F\in\mathcal I_\kappa(G)\), then
\[
tc(G-F)>\kappa.
\]

### Proof

If \(tc(G-F)\le\kappa\), then \(E(G)=F\cup E(G-F)\) is the union of two members of \(\mathcal I_\kappa(G)\). By T1 it belongs to \(\mathcal I_\kappa(G)\), contradicting \(tc(G)>\kappa\).

### Exact falsifier

A graph of triangle-cover number above κ that becomes κ-coverable after deleting a κ-coverable edge set.

## C2.1 — No Edge-Minimal High-Cover Graph

**Status:** `UNCONDITIONAL_COROLLARY`  
**Claim hash:** `6cf622ccae4ca082d23e80986b3f0fbcae374f49e177f48b7dbe316acd597c4e`  
**Lean mission:** `no_edgeMinimal_triangleCover_gt_kappa`  
**Novelty query:** `"edge-minimal" uncountable triangle cover`

### Statement

If \(\kappa\) is infinite and \(tc(G)>\kappa\), then deleting any single edge, any set of at most \(\kappa\) edges, or more generally any edge set of triangle-cover number at most \(\kappa\), leaves triangle-cover number greater than \(\kappa\). In particular, no such graph is edge-minimal for the property \(tc>\kappa\).

### Proof

A set of at most \(\kappa\) individual edges is covered by at most \(\kappa\) one-edge triangle-free layers. Apply T2.

### Exact falsifier

An edge-minimal graph with triangle-cover number above an infinite κ.

## T3 — Small-Vertex Deletion Persistence

**Status:** `UNCONDITIONAL_ELEMENTARY`  
**Claim hash:** `60234c81b65413cf19871eecf39e36ca94daa6794e2b3f7fcec7985b8917c4c3`  
**Lean mission:** `triangleCover_gt_delete_smallVertexSet`  
**Novelty query:** `"triangle cover" countable vertex deletion robustness`

### Statement

Let \(\kappa\) be infinite. If \(tc(G)>\kappa\) and \(S\subseteq V(G)\) has \(|S|\le\kappa\), then
\[
tc(G-S)>\kappa.
\]

### Proof

Let \(F_S\) be the set of all edges incident with at least one vertex of \(S\). It is covered by the \(|S|\) stars centered at vertices of \(S\), and every star is triangle-free. Hence \(F_S\in\mathcal I_\kappa(G)\). The spanning graph \(G-F_S\) is \(G-S\) together with isolated vertices from \(S\), so the two graphs have the same triangle-cover number. Apply T2.

### Exact falsifier

A graph with tc>κ and a vertex set of size at most κ whose deletion makes tc≤κ.

## T4 — Component Supremum Formula

**Status:** `UNCONDITIONAL_ELEMENTARY`  
**Claim hash:** `dfb1e949e5a9dc1ab5c92a06a3031917bcf2d190e6e1efa946f4bd301f730e15`  
**Lean mission:** `triangleCover_iUnion_components`  
**Novelty query:** `"triangle cover number" disjoint union supremum`

### Statement

If \(G\) is the disjoint union of its connected components \(\{G_i:i\in I\}\), then
\[
tc(G)=\sup_{i\in I} tc(G_i).
\]

### Proof

Restriction of any triangle-free cover of \(G\) to \(G_i\) proves \(tc(G_i)\le tc(G)\), giving the lower bound. For the upper bound, let \(\lambda=\sup_i tc(G_i)\). Color the edges of every component with the same palette \(\lambda\), using a nonmonochromatic-triangle coloring within that component. A triangle lies in one component, so the combined coloring is valid.

### Exact falsifier

A disjoint union whose triangle-cover number differs from the supremum of those of its components.

## T5 — Small-Separator Escape

**Status:** `UNCONDITIONAL_ELEMENTARY`  
**Claim hash:** `2f7fbaf90fae005ac419ddf27ce55b3aa519b3cacb01c1f53e8daa229e894466`  
**Lean mission:** `triangleCover_escape_component_after_smallSeparator`  
**Novelty query:** `"countable separator" triangle-cover escape component`

### Statement

Let \(\kappa\) be infinite. If \(tc(G)>\kappa\) and \(|S|\le\kappa\), then some connected component \(C\) of \(G-S\) satisfies
\[
tc(C)>\kappa.
\]

### Proof

By T3, \(tc(G-S)>\kappa\). If every component of \(G-S\) had triangle-cover number at most \(\kappa\), T4 would imply \(tc(G-S)\le\kappa\), a contradiction.

### Exact falsifier

A high-cover graph and small separator for which every residual component is κ-coverable.

## T6 — Ordered Forward-Link Coloring

**Status:** `UNCONDITIONAL_ELEMENTARY`  
**Claim hash:** `11b4669cf98a138524de9b2766856c77aec5d955204fd817eb256f5cdb8a3b72`  
**Lean mission:** `triangleCover_le_forwardLinkChromatic`  
**Novelty query:** `"forward neighborhood" chromatic number triangle-free edge cover`

### Statement

Let \(\prec\) be a well-order of \(V(G)\), and define
\[
N_\prec^+(v)=\{w\in N_G(v):v\prec w\}.
\]
If every forward link \(G[N_\prec^+(v)]\) is \(\kappa\)-colorable, then
\[
tc(G)\le\kappa.
\]

### Proof

For every \(v\), choose a proper vertex-coloring
\(\varphi_v:N_\prec^+(v)\to\kappa\). For an edge \(vw\) with \(v\prec w\), color \(vw\) by \(\varphi_v(w)\). Consider a triangle \(v\prec w\prec x\). The vertices \(w,x\) are adjacent inside \(G[N_\prec^+(v)]\), so \(\varphi_v(w)\ne\varphi_v(x)\). Thus the edges \(vw\) and \(vx\) have different colors, and the triangle is not monochromatic.

### Exact falsifier

A well-ordered graph with all forward links κ-colorable but tc>κ.

## T7 — High-Cover Link Extraction in \(K_4\)-Free Graphs

**Status:** `UNCONDITIONAL_ELEMENTARY`  
**Claim hash:** `79e9229b6d86279f07a06b52ecaae711c055cb8002dea2e3b6e9772ca566a284`  
**Lean mission:** `K4free_highTriangleCover_extracts_highChromaticLink`  
**Novelty query:** `"K4-free" high triangle cover forward link`

### Statement

Let \(G\) be \(K_4\)-free and let \(\kappa\) be a cardinal. If \(tc(G)>\kappa\), then for every well-order \(\prec\) of \(V(G)\), some vertex \(v\) satisfies
\[
\chi(G[N_\prec^+(v)])>\kappa,
\]
and this forward link is triangle-free.

### Proof

The chromatic conclusion is the contrapositive of T6. A triangle in \(N_G(v)\), together with \(v\), would form a \(K_4\); hence every full or forward link is triangle-free.

### Exact falsifier

A K4-free graph of tc>κ with a well-order whose every forward link is κ-colorable.

## T8 — Successor-Length Forward-Link Tower

**Status:** `UNCONDITIONAL_TRANSFINITE`  
**Claim hash:** `44272837f05ddb8629ea29446209a831d4faddff655b440da63230dddd88f326`  
**Lean mission:** `highTriangleCover_successorLength_linkTower`  
**Novelty query:** `"κ+ long" high chromatic neighborhood tower K4-free`

### Statement

Let \(\kappa\) be infinite and \(G\) be \(K_4\)-free with \(tc(G)>\kappa\). Then there is a sequence
\[
\langle (v_\alpha,L_\alpha):\alpha<\kappa^+\rangle
\]
such that the vertices \(v_\alpha\) are distinct and every \(L_\alpha\) is an induced triangle-free subgraph of \(G\) with
\[
L_\alpha\subseteq N_G(v_\alpha)
\quad\text{and}\quad
\chi(L_\alpha)>\kappa.
\]

### Proof

Proceed by transfinite recursion. At stage \(\alpha<\kappa^+\), let
\(S_\alpha=\{v_\beta:\beta<\alpha\}\). Since \(\alpha<\kappa^+\), \(|S_\alpha|\le\kappa\). By T3, \(G-S_\alpha\) still has triangle-cover number greater than \(\kappa\). Choose any well-order of its vertices and apply T7 to obtain a new apex \(v_\alpha\) and a high-chromatic triangle-free forward link \(L_\alpha\) in the residual. The apex is new because it lies outside \(S_\alpha\).

### Exact falsifier

A K4-free high-cover graph in which the recursion first fails at some ordinal below κ+.

## T9 — Stable Certification of Omitted Edges

**Status:** `UNCONDITIONAL_ELEMENTARY`  
**Claim hash:** `ecdddad03713d71272b30bb0f7ea1c49219fd320bd5d2a3b47f2fdf09de2c4d2`  
**Lean mission:** `maximalTriangleFree_omittedEdge_stableCertificate`  
**Novelty query:** `"maximal triangle-free subgraph" omitted edge stable common neighbors`

### Statement

Let \(G\) be \(K_4\)-free and \(H\) a maximal spanning triangle-free subgraph of \(G\). If \(xy\in E(G)\setminus E(H)\), define
\[
W_H(xy)=N_H(x)\cap N_H(y).
\]
Then \(W_H(xy)\) is nonempty and stable in \(G\).

### Proof

Maximality of \(H\) implies that adding \(xy\) creates a triangle, so some \(z\) satisfies \(xz,yz\in E(H)\); hence \(W_H(xy)\ne\varnothing\). If distinct \(z,z'\in W_H(xy)\) were adjacent in \(G\), then \(x,y,z,z'\) would have all six edges: \(xy,zz'\in E(G)\), and the four cross-edges lie in \(H\). This is a \(K_4\), contradiction.

### Exact falsifier

A maximal triangle-free spanning subgraph with an omitted edge having no common H-neighbor, or with two adjacent certificate vertices.

## T10 — Common Omitted-Edge Stable Book

**Status:** `UNCONDITIONAL_ELEMENTARY`  
**Claim hash:** `bd39dcc8485deef3d60d6a18b5dc67446c77f357ed667f3780900cb3422b3bfe`  
**Lean mission:** `highTriangleCover_commonOmittedEdge_stableBooks`  
**Novelty query:** `"common omitted edge" maximal triangle-free layers stable book`

### Statement

Let \(\kappa\) be infinite, \(G\) be \(K_4\)-free, and \(tc(G)>\kappa\). For every family
\[
\{H_i:i\in I\}
\]
of maximal spanning triangle-free subgraphs with \(|I|\le\kappa\), there is an edge \(xy\in E(G)\) omitted by every \(H_i\). For each \(i\), \(W_{H_i}(xy)\) is a nonempty stable subset of \(N_G(x)\cap N_G(y)\).

### Proof

If no edge were omitted by all \(H_i\), then the edge sets \(E(H_i)\) would cover \(E(G)\), contradicting \(tc(G)>\kappa\). Choose a common omitted edge and apply T9 to each layer.

### Exact falsifier

At most κ maximal triangle-free layers with no common omitted edge, or a common omitted edge lacking a stable certificate in one layer.

## T11 — Stable Triangle-Book Sterility

**Status:** `UNCONDITIONAL_ELEMENTARY`  
**Claim hash:** `b2d8c2c1fc4346f8c6fa98b499e4e9699c5f8e80b915a79e46a17278623db6e7`  
**Lean mission:** `stableTriangleBook_triangleCover_eq_two`  
**Novelty query:** `"stable triangle book" triangle cover number two`

### Statement

Let \(x,y\) be adjacent and let \(W\subseteq N(x)\cap N(y)\) be a nonempty stable set. Let \(B(xy,W)\) be the graph with edge set
\[
\{xy\}\cup\{xw,yw:w\in W\}.
\]
Then
\[
tc(B(xy,W))=2.
\]

### Proof

The graph contains a triangle \(xyw\), so its triangle-cover number is greater than one. It is covered by two triangle-free layers:
\[
\{xy\}\cup\{xw:w\in W\}
\quad\text{and}\quad
\{yw:w\in W\}.
\]
The first is a star with one additional incident edge \(xy\), and the second is a star; neither contains a triangle.

### Exact falsifier

A nonempty stable book that is triangle-free or requires at least three triangle-free layers.

## T13 — ω₁ Disjoint Critical-Cone Packing

**Status:** `UNCONDITIONAL_WITH_STANDARD_COMPACTNESS`  
**Claim hash:** `e6fea09897b280e0dc5a6d7f2c62dbe8fd49d2576fb6f26330405d06289c1dbf`  
**Lean mission:** `uncountableTriangleCover_disjointCriticalConePacking`  
**Novelty query:** `"ω1 many disjoint induced cones" critical triangle-free graphs`

### Statement

Let \(G\) be \(K_4\)-free with \(tc(G)>\aleph_0\), and let
\(f:\omega_1\to\omega\). Then \(G\) contains pairwise vertex-disjoint induced subgraphs
\[
K_1\vee Q_\alpha\qquad(\alpha<\omega_1),
\]
where every \(Q_\alpha\) is finite, triangle-free, vertex-critical, and
\[
\chi(Q_\alpha)>f(\alpha).
\]

### Proof

At stage \(\alpha<\omega_1\), fewer than \(\omega_1\) finite cones have been chosen, so their union of vertices is countable. Delete it; T3 leaves a \(K_4\)-free graph of uncountable triangle-cover number. By T7 it has an uncountably chromatic triangle-free link \(L\) at an apex \(v\). By the de Bruijn–Erdős compactness theorem for finite colorings, \(L\) has a finite induced subgraph of chromatic number greater than \(f(\alpha)\). Choose one minimal by vertex deletion and call it \(Q_\alpha\); it is vertex-critical. Since \(Q_\alpha\subseteq N(v)\), the vertices \(\{v\}\cup V(Q_\alpha)\) induce the cone \(K_1\vee Q_\alpha\). The prior deletion makes the cones pairwise vertex-disjoint.

### Exact falsifier

A first stage below ω1 at which the countable deletion, link extraction, finite compactness, or induced-cone step fails.

## C14 — Conditional ω₁ Cone Packing from Erdős #738

**Status:** `CONDITIONAL_ON_ERDOS_738_FOR_T`  
**Claim hash:** `d150e266637ebe7f84dd815f2b2eb77198301b74d2f5b12eb9e74908cfb8dff1`  
**Lean mission:** `erdos738_implies_omega1_disjointTreeCones_in_595Witness`  
**Novelty query:** `"Erdos 738" "ω1 disjoint cones" Erdős 595`

### Statement

Fix a finite tree \(T\). Assume every triangle-free graph of infinite chromatic number contains an induced copy of \(T\). Then every \(K_4\)-free graph \(G\) with \(tc(G)>\aleph_0\) contains \(\omega_1\) pairwise vertex-disjoint induced copies of
\[
K_1\vee T.

### Proof

Repeat the recursion in T13. After deleting the countable set of vertices used earlier, T3 and T7 produce an uncountably chromatic triangle-free link at a new apex. Apply the assumed Erdős #738 statement to find an induced \(T\) in that link, and adjoin the apex.

### Exact falsifier

A verified #738(T) theorem and a #595-type graph in which the disjoint-cone recursion fails.
